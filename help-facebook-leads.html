Connect Facebook & Instagram Lead Ads to your CRM

When someone submits your Facebook or Instagram lead form, the lead appears in your CRM automatically — no copy-paste, no spreadsheets. This is the full setup reference; the in-app Connect Facebook wizard walks the same steps with one-click buttons and automatic checks.

1 · Overview

What this connects and what you need before you start.

What it does: it links a Facebook Page's lead form to your CRM through your own Meta app. Every form submission (from Facebook or Instagram lead ads) is delivered to your CRM and lands in the right project for follow-up.

Everything happens in your own Meta account — your Business Manager, your Page, your app. The CRM only receives the leads. You never invent a "verify token": the CRM generates it for you and shows it when it's needed.

i
The CRM has a built-in Connect Facebook wizard that mirrors every step below with "Open in Meta" buttons, copy buttons and automatic checks. This page is the written reference for the same sequence.

You'll need

  • A Facebook Page (the one your leads come from).
  • A Meta Business Manager (business.facebook.com) where the Page lives.
  • A Meta app (Business type) — you'll create this in the steps.
  • Admin access to all of the above, and a personal Facebook login.
  • A company privacy policy URL (your website's /privacy page).

2 · Prerequisites checklist

Have these ready — most setup failures trace back to a missing item here.

  • A personal Facebook account you can log in with, and that is an admin of the Business Manager.
  • A Business Manager account (or access to your agency's — don't create a duplicate).
  • The Facebook Page added to that Business Manager.
  • A Lead Generation (Instant) form on the Page — at minimum Full name + Phone.
  • A Business-type Meta app (one app can serve all your Pages).
  • App permissions/products: Webhooks and Facebook Login for Business.
  • A public Privacy Policy URL (required to switch the app to Live).
  • Leads Access granted to the app on the Page (Business settings).

3 · Step-by-step

The same 12 steps as the in-app wizard. Steps 1–7 and 9 are done in Meta; step 8 is the one-click Connect in the CRM; steps 10–12 are automatic checks.

1

Create your Business Manager In Meta

business.facebook.com › Create account

Enter your business name, your name and a work email, then Submit. This is the home for your Page, ad account and app.

Already have one (maybe your ads agency made it)? Use it — just make sure you're an admin. Don't create a duplicate.

Screenshot: Business settings › Create account
2

Add or create your Facebook Page In Meta

Business settings › Pages › Add

Add your existing Page (claim it with Add a Page) or Create a new Page. Leads come from a Page's lead form, so the Page must belong to your Business Manager.

If the Page lives on a personal account, use Add a Page / request access rather than creating a new one — don't split your audience.

3

Create your lead form In Meta

Forms Library or Ads Manager › Instant Form

Create a Lead Generation Instant Form for your Page — the form people fill in. Build it in the Forms Library, or while creating a Lead ad in Ads Manager.

Example: keep it simple — at least Full name and Phone fields so leads import cleanly.

Screenshot: Instant Form builder with Full name + Phone
4

Create your Meta app In Meta

developers.facebook.com › My Apps › Create App

The app is the secure bridge between your Page and the CRM.

  1. Open My Apps › Create App (finish developer sign-up if prompted).
  2. Choose use case Other › app type Business.
  3. Name it (e.g. your company) and pick your Business Manager from Step 1.
  4. Click Create app.

Already have a Business-type app for ads? Use that one — don't make a second. "Create App" greyed out? Complete developer-account verification first.

5

Add the tools your app needs In Meta

App dashboard › Add product

Add two products (click Set up on each):

  • Webhooks — lets Facebook notify your CRM of new leads.
  • Facebook Login for Business — lets you connect your Page in one click at Step 8.

Just add them here — the settings come in the next steps.

6

Add privacy policy & go Live In Meta

App Settings › Basic  ·  App Mode toggle
  1. App Settings › Basic — paste your Privacy Policy URL and Save changes.
  2. Top of the dashboard — switch App Mode from Development to Live.
!
In Development mode Facebook will not send real leads. The Live toggle won't turn on until a Privacy Policy URL is saved.
7

Link your Page & grant Leads Access In Meta

Business settings › Pages › your Page › connect app + Leads Access

Connect your app to the Page and allow it to read the Page's leads.

How: Business settings › Pages › select your Page › Connected assets / Add assets › add your app. Then on the same Page, enable Leads Access (Lead Access) for the app.

Your app can only read a Page's leads after the Page is linked to it and Leads Access is granted.

Screenshot: Page › Connected assets › add app + Leads Access toggle
8

Connect Facebook In the CRM

Connect Facebook wizard › Step 8

This is the one-click connection — and the step that creates your verify token.

  1. If the wizard asks for app keys, paste your App ID and App Secret (from App Settings › Basic) and Save.
  2. Whitelist the CRM redirect in your app: Facebook Login for Business › Settings › Valid OAuth Redirect URIs — add the redirect URI shown in Technical values.
  3. Press Connect Facebook › sign in with your Facebook › grant the permissions › pick the Page › choose the project its leads belong to.
✓
The CRM stores the access on its server — you never copy a page token. It also auto-subscribes your Page to leadgen and generates the Verify Token you'll use in Step 9.
9

Turn on lead notifications In Meta

App › Webhooks › Page › leadgen › Subscribe

Open Webhooks › the Page object › find leadgen › switch Subscribe ON. When asked, paste the two values from your wizard (see Technical values):

  • Callback URL — your CRM's address (Copy in the wizard).
  • Verify Token — generated by the CRM at Step 8; now shown in the wizard, ready to copy.
!
If the Verify Token looks empty, you haven't connected yet — finish Step 8 (Connect Facebook) first, then return here; it's created at connect-time.
10

Connection health Automatic

The CRM checks the token is valid and shows which App ID it belongs to. Red means the login didn't grant all permissions or the token expired — repeat Step 8, accepting every permission.

11

Facebook can reach your CRM Automatic

When you subscribed at Step 9, Facebook sends a one-time verification ping. The CRM confirms it arrived. If not, re-check that leadgen is Subscribed and the Callback URL matches, then press Subscribe again.

12

Send a test lead In Meta › CRM detects

developers.facebook.com › Lead Ads Testing Tool

Pick your Page + form, Create Lead, and wait for Success. The CRM detects the first lead and ticks this green. Delete the test lead afterwards. (More in Testing.)

✓
When all 12 are green: "Connected & verified — your leads will now arrive automatically." A health strip on the Page's row (token valid, last event, lead count) stays visible so you can spot problems later.

5 · Instagram lead ads

Good news: Instagram lead ads need no separate setup.

Instagram lead ads arrive through the same "Connect Facebook" connection you set up above — there is nothing extra to configure. The only requirement is that your Instagram account is linked to the Facebook Page (Page settings › Linked accounts › Instagram). Lead-form submissions from Instagram then flow into the CRM exactly like Facebook ones.

i
Instagram Direct Messages are a different feature, set up on a separate screen. Instagram doesn't share a phone number for DMs, so the message and the sender's handle come through and follow-up happens in Instagram. DM setup is optional and unrelated to lead ads.

6 · Domain setup & going Live

Three things in App Settings › Basic let the connection work and go Live.

  • App Domains — add nexunova.com and supabase.co. These authorise the CRM's domains so the OAuth pop-up and webhook aren't blocked.
  • Privacy Policy URL — a public page (your website's /privacy). Required before the app can go Live.
  • App Mode = Live — top of the dashboard. In Development mode Facebook never sends real leads.

Separately, in Facebook Login for Business › Settings, add the Valid OAuth Redirect URI exactly as shown — this is what lets the Connect pop-up return to the CRM.

!
The redirect URI must match character-for-character (including https:// and the trailing path). A mismatch is the #1 cause of the "URL blocked" error.

7 · Common errors & fixes

Almost every issue is one of these.

✗ "URL blocked: This redirect failed" in the Connect pop-up
The redirect URI isn't whitelisted. In your app › Facebook Login for Business › Settings › Valid OAuth Redirect URIs, add https://rms.nexunova.com/fb-oauth-callback.html exactly, Save, and try again.
✗ "Domain not included in the app's domains" / can't switch to Live
In App Settings › Basic, add nexunova.com and supabase.co under App Domains, and make sure a Privacy Policy URL is saved. Then toggle App Mode › Live.
✗ The Verify Token field is empty at Step 9
The token is created when you connect. Finish Step 8 (Connect Facebook) first, then return to Step 9 — the token will be populated and ready to copy.
✗ Token invalid or expired (Step 10 red)
Re-connect: Step 8 › Connect Facebook again, accepting every permission in the pop-up.
✗ Test lead stuck on "Pending" forever
Your app isn't Live (Step 6) or leadgen isn't Subscribed (Step 9). Fix both, then create a fresh test lead.
✗ No leads ever arrive (Step 11/12 never go green)
Your Page isn't linked to the app, or Leads Access isn't granted (Step 7). Open Business settings › Pages › your Page, connect your app, and enable lead access. Also confirm leadgen is subscribed (Step 9).
✗ Leads connect but go to the wrong person
Routing to the receiving director is set by your CRM administrator and can't be changed from the operator screen by design. Contact your admin.

8 · Testing

Confirm a real lead flows end-to-end before you rely on it.

  1. Open the Lead Ads Testing Tool — developers.facebook.com/tools/lead-ads-testing.
  2. Select your Page and the lead form from Step 3.
  3. Click Create Lead (then Preview form › submit). Wait for Success.
  4. In the CRM wizard, Step 12 ticks green automatically when the lead arrives, and the lead appears in the project you chose at Step 8.
  5. Delete the test lead in the tool afterwards.
Screenshot: Lead Ads Testing Tool › Create Lead › Success
i
If the test never reaches the CRM, re-read Common errors — it's almost always App not Live, leadgen not subscribed, or Leads Access not granted.

9 · FAQ

Do I have to create or copy a "verify token"?
No. The CRM generates it for you at Connect (Step 8). You only copy it from the wizard into Meta at Step 9.
Can one Meta app serve several Pages?
Yes. Create the app once; you can connect multiple Pages, each mapped to its own project, in one Connect step.
Where is the Callback URL for my specific tenant?
It's shown in your own wizard at Step 9 (the project reference can differ between tenants). Copy it from there rather than hard-coding the one on this page.
Does Instagram need separate setup?
For lead ads, no — they arrive through the same Facebook connection, as long as the Instagram account is linked to the Page. Instagram Direct Messages are a separate, optional feature.
Do I need to keep the Connect pop-up or browser open after connecting?
No. Once Step 8 succeeds the access is stored on the CRM server. Leads keep arriving even when you're logged out.
Who can run this, and who can see the connection?
The operator (IT / lead-entry role) runs the whole wizard — connect, test, view logs. Directors get a read-only view of connected Pages and health; leads still route to the active director automatically.
The wizard asks for an App ID and App Secret — where are those?
In your Meta app › App Settings › Basic (click Show on the secret). The CRM keeps the secret on its server and never displays it again.
