Stop chasing spreadsheets. The recovery radar streams every overdue installment, every captured promise and every payment in real time. Filter by project, agent, region or aging bucket — act in seconds, not days.

  • Live overdue feed with aging buckets (1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ days)
  • Agent-wise recovery scoreboard and SLA tracking
  • Promise-vs-payment delta surface for daily ops standups
  • Smart prioritisation by amount, age and probability

Smart WhatsApp Payment Links

Replace bank challans, IBAN copy-paste pain and email PDFs with a single tap. Generate a pre-authenticated payment link, send over WhatsApp, and watch the payment, receipt and ledger entry happen automatically.

  • One-tap link generation from any installment
  • Bank, wallet and card payment options auto-selected
  • Real-time delivery, open and payment status
  • Auto-issued digital receipt with QR validation
  • Direct ledger posting — no manual reconciliation

5-Dimensional Ledger System

Every transaction in RMS is simultaneously posted across five ledger dimensions — so you can answer any question, from any angle, instantly. No more spreadsheet hunts, no more disputes.

  • Client Ledger — complete buyer position with every installment & payment
  • Agent Ledger — commission accrual, payouts and clawbacks
  • Project Ledger — project-level cash inflow and overdue summary
  • Unit Ledger — per-unit financial state for transfer and resale
  • Officer Ledger — collection accountability by officer
