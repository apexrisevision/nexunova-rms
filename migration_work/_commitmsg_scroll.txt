sales-portal: lock background scroll when a modal is open (no scroll bleed)

Reserve/info sheets are short, so the modal itself rarely scrolls — the gesture
was bleeding through to the availability board behind it. Now openModal locks
the page (saves scrollY, position:fixed body) and closeModal restores it; the
modal gets overscroll-behavior:contain so boundary scrolls don't chain either.
