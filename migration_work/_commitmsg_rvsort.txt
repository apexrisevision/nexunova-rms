Receipt Vouchers: sort date-wise (newest first) — RPC had no ORDER BY

list_payments_filtered returned heap order, so Receipt Vouchers and the
Receipts report appeared randomly sorted; the LIMIT could also silently drop
the most recent vouchers. Added ORDER BY payment_date DESC, created_at DESC,
id DESC in both the inner (LIMIT-bearing) subquery and jsonb_agg. Verified on
Fourteen Group: output now strictly date-descending.
