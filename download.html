Why Desktop

Everything the web version has,
built for Windows

The desktop app is a native Windows shell around the live Nexunova RMS platform — giving you a dedicated, distraction-free workspace that stays signed in and always ready.

No Browser Required
Runs as a standalone Windows app. No browser tab clutter, no accidental closures, no competing with other tabs for RAM.
System Tray Integration
RMS lives in your Windows taskbar tray. One click to open, hide, or jump straight to the recovery radar — even when the window is minimised.
Native Windows Notifications
Get real Windows push notifications for overdue instalments, pending promises, and agent check-ins — even when RMS is minimised to tray.
Automatic Updates
Always on the latest version of RMS. The app silently checks for updates in the background and applies them on next launch — zero manual work.
Session Persistence
Stay signed in indefinitely. No browser cookie expiry, no "you were signed out" surprises mid-recovery-call. Your session is yours.
Secure & Isolated
No shared browser history. No extensions reading your RMS data. The desktop app runs in an isolated Chromium sandbox — your recovery data stays private.
Setup Guide

Up and running in under 2 minutes

Three steps. No IT department required.

01
Download the Installer
Click the Download button at the top. Your browser will save NexunovaRMS-Setup.exe — ~95 MB, digitally signed by Nexunova.
02
Run the Setup
Double-click the downloaded file. If Windows SmartScreen appears, click More info → Run anyway. The installer completes in under a minute.
03
Sign In & Work
Log in with your existing Nexunova RMS credentials. All your projects, clients, ledgers, and payment links load automatically. Done.
